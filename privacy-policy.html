Privacy Policy

Your privacy is central to AirAI. Because AirAI operates as a client-side web application without a custom application database, your chat history and custom memories remain in your own browser.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

AirAI uses your browser's localStorage solely to persist your experience across sessions:

  • myai_chats_v1 — Your conversation threads on this device.
  • myai_settings_v1 — Language, theme, speech rate, voice, and research toggles.
  • myai_memory_v1 — Custom offline answers you explicitly teach AirAI.
  • myai_learned_v1 — Topic summaries learned during Browser Research when Auto-Learn is on.
  • myai_consent_v1 — Your cookie and privacy notice preference.

We do not upload, sell, or store your private chat logs on a central AirAI server. You can delete any or all saved chats, custom memories, and learned topics at any time using the Settings & Custom Memory modal or your browser's site data controls.

2. Voice Input & Speech Synthesis

Voice input uses your browser's native Web Speech API (SpeechRecognition) only when you click the microphone button and grant browser permission. Audio processing is handled by your browser vendor's speech service according to their privacy policy. Text-to-speech uses your device's built-in speechSynthesis engine.

3. External Requests During Browser Research

When Browser Research Mode is enabled and you submit a query while online, your browser directly requests factual summaries from public, CORS-enabled APIs (Wikipedia REST/Search API and Free Dictionary API). You may disable Browser Research Mode anytime in Settings or via the globe toggle in the chat composer.

4. Google AdSense, Cookies & Third-Party Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other third-party ad networks. In accordance with Google AdSense policies:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Users can also opt out of third-party vendor cookie usage for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info and reviewing How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

5. Children's Privacy

AirAI is intended for general educational and productivity use and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes & Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements or legal requirements. Questions or privacy requests can be submitted via our Contact page.
