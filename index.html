How does MyAI work without a backend server?

Built-in knowledge, math evaluation, unit conversions, and your Custom Memory run directly in JavaScript inside your browser. Chat history is stored locally on your device.

Where do Browser Research answers come from?

When Browser Research Mode is active and you are online, MyAI queries open Wikipedia and Free Dictionary endpoints directly from your browser and includes clickable source links.

How can I manage or delete my saved data?

Open Settings or Custom Memory from the sidebar to clear conversations, remove learned browser topics, or update your cookie and privacy preferences anytime.
