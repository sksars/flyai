About MyAI — Privacy-First Offline & Browser Research Assistant

MyAI is a free, lightweight, serverless web assistant designed to help students, developers, and everyday users solve math, write code, learn new topics, and interact by voice across multiple languages directly inside their browser.

Why MyAI Exists

Many modern AI tools require paid accounts, constant server round-trips, or heavy data tracking. MyAI takes a local-first approach: core capabilities—such as offline knowledge guides, custom user-taught memory, unit conversions, and arithmetic—run locally inside your browser without requiring any backend server.

Core Capabilities

  • Offline Local Storage Architecture: Conversations, preferences, custom notes, and learned summaries are persisted exclusively in your browser's localStorage.
  • Context-Aware Browser Research: When online with Browser Research Mode enabled, MyAI can look up live, source-cited factual summaries from open knowledge APIs (Wikipedia and Free Dictionary API) and understand follow-up questions within the active conversation.
  • Auto-Learn for Future Offline Use: Verified research summaries can be saved locally so MyAI remembers how to answer those topics even when your internet connection is offline.
  • Multilingual Interface & Voice Accessibility: Supports English, हिन्दी (Hindi), Hinglish, Español, Français, and العربية with Web Speech voice recognition and text-to-speech playback.

Open Content & Attribution

Live encyclopedia summaries retrieved through Browser Research originate from Wikipedia contributors and are linked directly to their source articles under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) license. Word definitions are retrieved from Free Dictionary API. MyAI always displays source links alongside researched answers for verification and transparency.
